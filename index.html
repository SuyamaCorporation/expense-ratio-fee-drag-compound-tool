Key Insights

The Compounding Effect

Fees compound just like returns — a 1% fee doesn't just cost 1% of gains. It reduces your compounding base each year, causing exponential drag over time.

The 1% Rule

A 1% expense ratio on a 30-year investment often erodes 20-25% of your final portfolio value — not 30% of gains. The "fee" is far larger than it appears.

Index Fund Advantage

Most actively managed funds charge 0.5%–2%+ annually, yet consistently underperform index funds net of fees. Low-cost indexing captures market returns efficiently.
